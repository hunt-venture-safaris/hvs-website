> **In short:** Trophy hunting in Pakistan is legal under government quotas and permits, and visiting hunters book through a licensed Pakistani outfitter. Hunt Venture Safaris (Pvt) Limited, Govt. Licence No. 2087, arranges the permit, the firearm documents and your travel within Pakistan. These guides explain each step.

Hunts take place in Gilgit-Baltistan for [Astore Markhor](/hunts/astore-markhor/), [Himalayan Ibex](/hunts/himalayan-ibex/) and [Blue Sheep](/hunts/blue-sheep/), and in southern Pakistan for the [Blanford Urial and Sindh Ibex combination](/hunts/blanford-urial-sindh-ibex/) and [Punjab Urial](/hunts/punjab-urial/).

## Permits and CITES

Permits are allocated to Pakistani outfitters through an open government auction, within national quotas. Your permit and provincial shooting licence fee are included in our packages. Most species are CITES-listed, and markhor is Appendix I, so your country may need an import permit. [Read the permits and CITES guide](/hunting-in-pakistan/permits-and-cites/).

## Firearms

You can bring your own rifle. Firearm import and export documentation is included; we need your rifle and passport details early. [Read the firearms guide](/hunting-in-pakistan/firearms/).

## Trophy export

We skin, salt and pack your trophy and handle the permit formalities. Export, shipment and customs clearance are arranged and paid separately. [Read the trophy export guide](/hunting-in-pakistan/trophy-export/).

## Travel and logistics

Mountain hunts start in Islamabad, with an included flight to Gilgit and 4x4 transport if it is cancelled. The Dureji hunt starts in Karachi. [Read the travel and logistics guide](/hunting-in-pakistan/travel-and-logistics/).

## Season dates

Legal and recommended periods for every hunt in 2027–28, in one table. [Read the season dates guide](/hunting-in-pakistan/hunting-seasons/).

## Before you book

- Choose your species and check the recommended period.
- Ask your national CITES authority what your trophy needs to enter your country.
- Check your passport has at least six months' validity and look up the visa rules for your nationality.
- Arrange travel, medical and evacuation insurance; it is not included.
- Send us your rifle details if you plan to bring one.
- Note our terms: 40% in advance, balance before the hunt starts. Rates for 2027–28 are on request.
