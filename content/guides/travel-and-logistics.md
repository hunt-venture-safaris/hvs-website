> **In short:** For hunts in Gilgit-Baltistan you arrive in Islamabad; we meet you and fly you to Gilgit on the included return flight. If the mountain flight is cancelled, which happens in bad weather, we drive you by 4x4 at no extra cost. For the Blanford Urial and Sindh Ibex hunt you arrive in Karachi. Insurance and rescue cover are not included, so arrange your own.

## Arriving in Islamabad

The Astore Markhor, Himalayan Ibex and Blue Sheep hunts start and end in Islamabad. Book your international flight into Islamabad International Airport for day 1 of the itinerary. We meet you on arrival, help with firearm formalities (see [Bringing your rifle to Pakistan](/hunting-in-pakistan/firearms/)) and take you to your hotel. Airport transfers, airport tax, road tolls, entry and parking fees are all included.

## Islamabad–Gilgit flights and the 4x4 fallback

Return flights between Islamabad and Gilgit are included. On day 2 you fly to Gilgit, where we complete your trophy permit formalities.

The flight crosses high mountains and depends on clear weather, so cancellations are not unusual, especially in winter. When a flight is cancelled, 4x4 transport is included and we drive instead. The road journey follows the Karakoram Highway and takes most of a day or longer, depending on road and weather conditions.

Practical advice:

- allow a spare day at the end of your trip before your international flight home
- book international tickets that can be changed
- keep essentials and medication in your hand luggage in case of a road journey

## Arriving in Karachi (southern hunts)

The [Blanford Urial and Sindh Ibex hunt](/hunts/blanford-urial-sindh-ibex/) runs Karachi to Karachi over 9 days. We meet you at Karachi airport on day 1 and transfer you to the hotel; on day 2 you leave for the hunting area in Dureji, Sindh–Balochistan. Accommodation in Karachi and at the hunting camps, Karachi airport transfers and a domestic return flight are included.

For the [Punjab Urial hunt](/hunts/punjab-urial/) (Kala Bagh, Jhelum, Padri), the start and end city is on request.

## Baggage

The package includes baggage of up to 50 kg or two duffle bags. Excess baggage is not included and is charged by the airline. Domestic flights to Gilgit use smaller aircraft, so pack in soft duffles rather than hard suitcases where you can.

We provide tents, cutlery, safety and camping equipment. Personal clothing and equipment are yours to bring.

## Accommodation and meals (full board)

All packages are full board: meals, tea and coffee, and mineral and treated drinking water. In Gilgit-Baltistan you stay in Gilgit and at hunting camps in the community-controlled hunting area; a shikar guide, cook and support staff travel with you, alongside your licensed mountain guide and professional hunter. Altitudes on the mountain hunts range from 2,500 m to 4,700 m; the Blue Sheep hunt includes a two-day trek in and out of the Shimshal Valley.

Personal expenses (calls, laundry, drinks, room service, à la carte meals) and gratuities for guides, cooks and staff are not included.

## Visa assistance

Pakistan visa assistance is included. Most visitors apply online through the Government of Pakistan's official portal, [visa.nadra.gov.pk](https://visa.nadra.gov.pk); eligibility and documents vary by nationality, and your passport should be valid for at least six months ([Embassy of Pakistan, Paris](https://mofa.gov.pk/paris-e-visa)). We provide the supporting letter you need from us. Check the current rules for your nationality before you apply.

## Insurance and rescue (not included)

Personal insurance and medication, and ground or helicopter rescue, are not included. Take out travel and medical insurance that covers hunting at altitude up to 4,700 m, evacuation and trip cancellation. A first aid kit is carried on every hunt.

## Payment

Payment terms are 40% in advance, with the balance due before the hunt starts.
