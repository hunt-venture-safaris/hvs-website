# Logos

- `hvs-logo.png` — The master logo, full horizontal lockup: "HVS" monogram in graphite (`graphite`, #5A5A5D) with a rearing markhor rising from the V, crimson horn tips (`crimson`, #BD2030), and the wordmark "HUNT VENTURE SAFARIS / (PVT) LIMITED" in black serif capitals. Transparent PNG, 985 × 324 px. It is the only master: a vector (SVG) version is pending from the client.
- Dark-on-light only. On the light theme it sits directly on `field` (bone). On every dark ground (`field`, `panel`, photographs) it sits on the bone plate: `plate` fill, `radius-2` corners, `space-1` vertical and `space-3` horizontal padding (the `hvs-plate` class). Never place it straight on a dark ground, never invert or recolour it, never apply a glass or blur behind it.
- Minimum height 36px on screen (the wordmark's "(PVT) LIMITED" stops reading below that); below 36px, use the monogram crop from the brand guidelines instead.
- Clear space: at least the height of the "H" on every side, inside the plate.

## hvs-mark.png

The compact mark: the HVS monogram with the rearing markhor, cropped from the master without the wordmark (337×319, transparent). Use it for the favicon, social avatars and the mobile navigation bar. Same ink rules as the full logo: graphite and black with crimson horn tips, dark-on-light only, so place it on the `plate` colour on dark grounds.
