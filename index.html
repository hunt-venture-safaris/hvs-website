The expedition

From Islamabad to base camp, handled.

We take care of permits, rifles, transport and camp. You arrive fit and ready to climb.

  1. Permit and paperwork

    We secure your hunting permit and the temporary import licence for your rifle, and send your visa invitation letter.

  2. Arrive in Islamabad

    Met at the airport, rifle cleared through customs, overnight at a hotel.

  3. Fly or drive to Gilgit

    A short flight past Nanga Parbat, or the Karakoram Highway by road if weather grounds the plane.

  4. Into the valley

    Transfer to the hunting area, meet the community guides and set up base camp.

  5. The hunt

    Glassing at first light, stalks on foot with experienced local guides and porters.

  6. Trophy and export

    Field care, the export permit and shipping of your trophy, then the return to Islamabad.

Conservation

The trophy fee keeps the herd on the mountain.

Trophy hunting in Gilgit-Baltistan runs through community-managed conservancies. Most of each permit fee goes to the valley where the animal was taken.

  • Paid to the communityFees fund village game watchers, schools and water schemes in the hunting valley.
  • Old males onlyHunts target mature, past-breeding males, chosen with the local guides.
  • Strict quotasPermits are limited each season by the wildlife authorities based on herd surveys.
