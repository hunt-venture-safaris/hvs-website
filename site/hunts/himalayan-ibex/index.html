Terrain and altitude

Altitude2,500 – 4,700 m

Himalayan ibex hold on steep rock and scree between 2,500 and 4,700 m in Upper Hunza. Hunting is mostly glassing from high ground followed by stalks across rock and snow.

Getting there

Hunts in Gilgit-Baltistan start and finish in Islamabad. We meet you at the airport, fly you to Gilgit and back (return flights are included), and drive you to the hunting area. Flights between Islamabad and Gilgit depend on the weather; if they are cancelled, we drive the Karakoram Highway in our own 4x4 transport, which is also included.

Community-controlled hunting areas

Our hunts take place in community-controlled hunting areas. Permits are issued by the government and allocated to Pakistani outfitters through an open auction, and every hunt contributes to the conservation of the species and to the local communities who protect it.

Questions about this hunt

When is the best time to hunt Himalayan ibex?

The legal hunting period is 1 November to 26 April. We recommend mid November to mid February.

Where does the ibex hunt take place?

In District Hunza (Upper Hunza), Gilgit-Baltistan, in a community-controlled hunting area.

How many hunting days are there?

The 10-day plan includes five days of hunting. The hunt can run 10 to 12 days; extra hunting days can be added on request.

Can I combine ibex with another species?

Yes. Hunters often combine Himalayan ibex with markhor or blue sheep. Tell us what you have in mind and we will plan the dates.
