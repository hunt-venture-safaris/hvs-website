Terrain and altitude

Altitude2,500 – 4,700 m

Astore markhor live on steep cliffs, rocky slopes and forest edges between 2,500 and 4,700 m. Expect long climbs and stalks at altitude on most days; the 15 hunting days give time for weather and for finding the right animal.

Getting there

Hunts in Gilgit-Baltistan start and finish in Islamabad. We meet you at the airport, fly you to Gilgit and back (return flights are included), and drive you to the hunting area. Flights between Islamabad and Gilgit depend on the weather; if they are cancelled, we drive the Karakoram Highway in our own 4x4 transport, which is also included.

Community-controlled hunting areas

Our hunts take place in community-controlled hunting areas. Permits are issued by the government and allocated to Pakistani outfitters through an open auction, and every hunt contributes to the conservation of the species and to the local communities who protect it.

Questions about this hunt

When is the best time to hunt Astore markhor?

The legal hunting period is 15 October to 30 April. We recommend mid November to mid February.

How long is the markhor hunt?

20 days from Islamabad to Islamabad, with 15 full hunting days in the community-controlled hunting area.

Is the markhor permit included?

Yes. The trophy hunting permit for one Astore markhor and the provincial shooting licence fee are included. Permits are allocated to Pakistani outfitters through an open government auction.

Is trophy export included?

Trophy skinning, salting and packing and the trophy permit formalities are included. Trophy export from Pakistan, shipment and customs clearance are arranged separately.
