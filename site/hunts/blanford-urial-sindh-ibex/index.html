Terrain and altitude

500 – 3,000 ft (flat terrain)

Dureji lies between 500 and 3,000 ft, in dry, broken hills on the border of Sindh and Balochistan. The terrain is far easier than our mountain hunts, which makes this a good first hunt in Pakistan or a pairing with a northern species.

Getting there

The Dureji hunt starts and finishes in Karachi. We meet you at the airport, look after your firearm paperwork and drive you to the hunting camp. A domestic return flight is included.

Community-controlled hunting areas

Our hunts take place in community-controlled hunting areas. Permits are issued by the government and allocated to Pakistani outfitters through an open auction, and every hunt contributes to the conservation of the species and to the local communities who protect it.

Questions about this hunt

When is the best time for the Dureji combination hunt?

The legal hunting period is 1 November to 30 April. We recommend December and January.

Where does the hunt start?

In Karachi. The hunt runs nine days, Karachi to Karachi, with accommodation in Karachi and at the hunting camp.

Are both permits included?

Yes. Trophy hunting permits for Blanford urial and Sindh ibex are included, with the provincial shooting licence fee.
