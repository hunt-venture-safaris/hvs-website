Hunting in Pakistan

Bringing your rifle to Pakistan

Most of our hunters travel with their own rifle. The Pakistani paperwork for bringing it in and taking it home is included in every package; your part is to send us the details early and to clear the rifle out of, and back into, your own country.

Updated 8 November 2026

In short: Yes, you can bring your own hunting rifle to Pakistan. Firearm import and export documentation is included in every Hunt Venture Safaris package, and we arrange it with the authorities before you travel. We need your rifle and passport details well in advance; you are responsible for your own country's export rules, your airline's firearm policy and any transit country's rules.

Firearm documents are included

"Firearm import/export documentation" is part of every package, together with government liaison for permits and documents. In practice this means:

  • we apply for the permission that allows your rifle to enter Pakistan temporarily for the hunt, based on the details you send us
  • we meet you at the airport on arrival and look after the firearm paperwork with you
  • the same documents cover the rifle's departure with you at the end of the hunt

Firearm documents are prepared for the specific rifle, serial number and traveller named on them. A rifle that does not match the paperwork cannot be cleared, so please tell us at once if anything changes.

What we need from you

Send these with your booking, or as soon as your travel is fixed:

  • a clear copy of your passport photo page
  • make, model, calibre and serial number of each rifle (and of the scope, if your home country records it)
  • a copy of your home-country firearm licence or registration
  • your flight details into and out of Pakistan
  • quantity and calibre of ammunition you plan to bring

When your hunt is confirmed we send you the full document checklist and the date we need it by.

Pakistan's rules for foreign visitors on hunting trips require travel details to be shared with the authorities ahead of arrival (Gulf News report on the 2017 SOP); sending everything early avoids delays at the airport.

Your side: home country, airline and transit

These are your responsibility, and the rules differ by country:

  • Leaving and returning home. Many countries require you to register or declare the rifle on departure so you can bring it back without question. For example, US travellers can register firearms with US Customs and Border Protection before travel. Check your own customs authority.
  • Your airline. Airlines set their own firearm rules: advance notice, locked hard case, ammunition packed separately and weight limits. Book the rifle with the airline when you book your seat.
  • Transit countries. If you change planes, the rifle may need permission to pass through the transit country, even airside. Prefer routes that let you check the rifle through to Islamabad or Karachi, and confirm with the airline.

Travelling via Islamabad or Karachi

Our Gilgit-Baltistan hunts (Astore Markhor, Himalayan Ibex, Blue Sheep) start and end in Islamabad. You clear the rifle on arrival at Islamabad, then fly with us to Gilgit on the included domestic flight, or travel by road with our 4x4 if the flight is cancelled. The Blanford Urial and Sindh Ibex hunt starts and ends in Karachi.

Baggage included in the package is up to 50 kg or two duffle bags; a rifle case may count towards this. See Travel and logistics.

Ammunition

Ask us about ammunition for your hunt: we confirm how much you may bring and how it is declared.

Until we confirm the details for your hunt, plan to bring only what you need, in its original packaging and packed as your airline requires.

Prefer not to travel with a rifle?

If you would rather not travel with a rifle, ask us about the options for your hunt.

If travelling with a firearm is impractical for you, tell us when you enquire and we will advise on the options for your hunt.

Related hunts: Astore Markhor, Himalayan Ibex, Blue Sheep, Blanford Urial and Sindh Ibex, Punjab Urial. See also Permits and CITES.
