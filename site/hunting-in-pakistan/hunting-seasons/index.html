Hunting season dates in Pakistan, 2027–28

Pakistan's trophy hunting season runs through the autumn, winter and early spring. Here are the legal and recommended periods for each of our hunts in 2027–28, with altitude, duration and start city.

Updated 8 November 2026

In short: For 2027–28, the Astore Markhor season runs 15 October – 30 April, Himalayan Ibex 1 November – 26 April, and Blue Sheep and the Blanford Urial and Sindh Ibex combination 1 November – 30 April. The best time for markhor and ibex is mid November to mid February; for Blue Sheep, November, March and April; for Dureji, December and January; and for Punjab Urial, December to March.

Season dates follow the official notification each season and may change. For reference, the Gilgit-Baltistan 2026–27 season was reported as 15 October 2026 to 10 April 2027 (Dawn, September 2026), which ends earlier than the 2027–28 dates above. We will confirm the dates for your hunt once the 2027–28 notification is issued.

Best time for each species

Astore Markhor (Capra falconeri falconeri). Hunted in Nagar, Skardu, Astore, Gilgit, Bunji and Kargah. The legal period opens on 15 October, but we recommend mid November to mid February. The 20-day itinerary gives 15 full hunting days, which leaves room for weather.

Himalayan Ibex (Capra ibex sibirica). Hunted in Upper Hunza, District Hunza, with the same recommended window as markhor: mid November to mid February. The plan has five hunting days within a 10–12 day trip.

Blue Sheep (Pseudois nayaur, also called bharal). Hunted in the Shimshal Valley, District Hunza, at 3,200–4,500 m. We recommend November, March and April, and the trip includes a two-day trek to the hunting area and two days out.

Blanford Urial and Sindh Ibex. A combination hunt in Dureji, on the Sindh–Balochistan border, at low altitude on flat terrain. December and January are recommended; the plan gives three days for Blanford Urial and two for Sindh Ibex.

Punjab Urial. Hunted in Kala Bagh, Jhelum and Padri, with up to five hunting days. December to March is recommended. Full details are on request.

Planning around the season

  • Markhor permits are few. In 2026–27 Gilgit-Baltistan auctioned three Astore Markhor permits for the whole season (Dawn). Enquire early if you want a markhor hunt in the recommended window.
  • Combining hunts. The mountain hunts share the Gilgit base and overlapping seasons, and the Dureji hunt is a low-altitude option in December and January. Ask us whether a combination works for your dates.
  • Allow for travel days. Islamabad–Gilgit flights are weather-dependent; we drive by 4x4 if they are cancelled. See Travel and logistics.
  • Import permits take time. If your trophy needs a home-country import permit, start the application once your dates are fixed. See Permits and CITES.

Weather and snow

The mountain hunts take place in winter in the Karakoram, Himalaya and Hindu Kush, between 2,500 m and 4,700 m. Expect cold conditions and snow at height; personal clothing and equipment are yours to bring, while tents and camping equipment are provided.

Weather varies by valley and by month. Ask us about typical conditions for your dates and we will advise on clothing and timing.
