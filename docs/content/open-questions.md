# Open questions for Hunt Venture Safaris

The live site avoids stating anything below as fact; where it matters, it tells visitors to ask. Answering these lets us make the pages more specific. Grouped by page.

## Planning guides

### Permits and CITES (`/hunting-in-pakistan/permits-and-cites/`)
1. How permits for the Sindh, Balochistan and Punjab hunts are allocated.
2. The CITES appendix for Blanford urial and Punjab urial (depends on subspecies naming). The CITES table on the page was built from the 2016 Appendices; please check it, or let us check it against Species+.
3. The exact passport and permit documents you need from hunters, and the deadline.
4. The lead time you advise for a hunter's home-country import permit (markhor especially).

### Trophy export (`/hunting-in-pakistan/trophy-export/`)
5. Do you clean skulls, or ship horns and skull as taken?
6. Which shipping or clearing agent do you use, and where are trophies sent from (Islamabad, Karachi or Gilgit)?
7. Can hunters carry a trophy as accompanied baggage, or must it go as air cargo?
8. Typical time from the end of the hunt to dispatch, and to arrival, for US and EU clients.

### Firearms (`/hunting-in-pakistan/firearms/`)
9. The name of the temporary firearm import document and the authority that issues it.
10. Do your staff meet hunters inside the arrivals or customs area? (The page says "we meet you at the airport".)
11. The full document checklist, how many rifles each hunter may bring, and the deadline.
12. Does the rifle go in the hold on the Islamabad–Gilgit flight? Any domestic airline rules?
13. Does the rifle case count within the 50 kg allowance?
14. Ammunition: how much may be brought, how it is declared, and whether it can be bought locally.
15. Do you offer rifle hire?

### Travel and logistics (`/hunting-in-pakistan/travel-and-logistics/`)
16. Is the Islamabad hotel night included? The brochure inclusions mention Gilgit and the camps only.
17. Current airline(s) and frequency on Islamabad–Gilgit.
18. The road time Islamabad–Gilgit you quote to clients.
19. Punjab urial: start and end city.
20. Do you issue an invitation or sponsor letter, and which visa category do you recommend?
21. How is a helicopter rescue arranged if needed, and is a deposit or guarantee required?

### Season dates (`/hunting-in-pakistan/hunting-seasons/`)
22. Check the 2027–28 dates against the official notification. Dawn reported the 2026–27 Gilgit-Baltistan season as 15 Oct – 10 Apr, which ends earlier than the brochure dates (ibex 26 Apr, blue sheep 30 Apr).
23. Why mid November – mid February is best for markhor and ibex (snow brings animals lower?).
24. Why mid-winter is not recommended for blue sheep (access, snow on the trek?).
25. Typical temperatures and snow by month for Astore, Hunza, Shimshal and Dureji.
26. Do you offer multi-species combinations beyond the Dureji combo?

## Spanish pages — for a native speaker
- "Cacería" (the product) vs "caza" (the activity); "periodo hábil" vs "temporada hábil".
- "Coto gestionado por la comunidad local" vs "área de caza comunitaria".
- "Organizador de caza" vs "outfitter"; "guía con licencia y cazador profesional" vs "PH".
- "Recechar", "otear"; "desollado, salado y embalaje del trofeo".
- Species names: "íbice" vs "cabra"; "carnero azul" vs "bharal".
- Inclusions, exclusions and the permit note should be checked by a professional translator before relying on them as terms.
